# El Fogón, el Tabernero del Inframundo, el Fuego Nuevo y la historia

> Texto movido literal desde el CLAUDE.md viejo el 2026-11-12 (en inglés, como estaba). El índice está en CLAUDE.md.

**El Fogón Eterno, the "Zona 0" (v0.11.0; `GameData/FogonRules.cs`, `Database/seed_fogon.sql` + `add_fogon.sql` — run `add_fogon.sql` on a live database BEFORE the v0.11 binary: the code reads `zones.kind`, `users.in_gate` and `users.gate_cleared` in every player/zone query)** — the door to the Fuego Nuevo reset (design: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-06-fuego-nuevo-design.md`; plan: `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-06-fogon-y-fuego-nuevo.md`).
It is a row of `zones` with `zone_id = 0` and `kind = 'gate'`; **`IZoneRepository.GetAllAsync` and `IMonsterRepository.GetAllAsync` return ONLY the normal zones/monsters** (the gate comes from `GetGateAsync` / `GetBossByZoneAsync(0)`), so the ladder, `ZoneRanking`, `/zonas`' normal rows, per-zone recipes, `/drops`, boxes, pets and the drop exchange never see it — never add a code path that iterates "all zones" to include it by accident, and the SQL scripts that loop over zones filter `zone_id > 0`
(renumber, the rework verifications, `report_box_economy`; `report_recipe_pacing -v gate=1` prints only the Fogón recipes). **A player never has `current_zone_id = 0`**: `/zona 0` only sets `users.in_gate` (their zone stays where it was), so everything keyed on the current zone keeps working; `ChangeZoneAsync` clears it (travelling anywhere normal leaves the gate). Rules: it opens when the player has beaten the boss of the LAST zone (`FogonRules.IsGateOpen`, by rank, never by raw id);
entering needs the gate's `min_level` (25) and the **Trinche del Asador Eterno** (+128 ATQ, no family) and **Brasa del Fogón Eterno** (+165 DEF since v0.15.1, it was +120) both EQUIPPED (the ×1.6 step over Zone 5 — gear lives in the slot, so the Zone 5 piece must be sold first); inside, `/hunt`, `/travel`, `/autohunt` and `/raid` answer `CombatStartStatus.InGate`/the raid rejection and `/boss` fights **El Asador Eterno** (the gear is re-checked at fight start: `GateGearMissing`, cooldown not spent; same 5 h / 30 min boss cooldown). Calibrated with the real `CombatTurnResolver` at level 28 (scratchpad `fogonsim`): Z5 boss ×1.3 HP / ×1.2 damage → ~16 % defeat with the Fogón gear, ~75 % with Zone 5's class weapon + amulet; bonus 1500/1500 until v0.13, **since v0.14.0 gold 3000 / XP 300** (the XP is wiped by the Fuego Nuevo, the gold is not) and its chest is the **Brasero del Fogón** (see "Free boxes per zone"); the first time per lap that a player beats it also hands the **6th pet egg** (`Huevo del Fogón` → `Chispa del Asador`, see Pets).
The recipes (zone 0, not affinity, 25.000 gold each) ask for the 10 HUNT drops of the 5 zones ×5 plus Madera de Ébano ×4 + Hierro ×18 / Gema de Zafiro ×4 + Carbón ×18 **plus, since v0.30.2, 300 Madera de Pino (the Trinche) / 300 Piedra (the Brasa)** (the owner: "necesito que pida mucha piedra y madera, tipo 300 y 300… una persona FN2 sacó 27 maderas recién": with the tools and the Fuego Nuevo gathering pays far more than when the recipes were calibrated; ~9 h of `/chop` or `/mine` per piece without a tool, ~3 h with the Zone 5 one; each piece now has 9 ingredients; `rebalance_gather_recipes.sql` for a live database — NEVER re-run `seed_fogon.sql` alone on one: it wipes the Asador's drop, the Brasero del Fogón, which `seed_zone_boxes.sql` hands out and which cannot be re-run after `retire_box_trophies.sql`), **plus, since v0.15.1, ONE Mythic gather item per piece** (Corteza del Árbol de Vida ×1 in the Trinche, Fragmento de Meteorito ×1 in the Brasa — the owner: "tienen que usarse para algo"; 0.5 % per action, ~1000 min each, in parallel because /chop and /mine have separate cooldowns, so they are found while the rest is done: simulated, 71 % of players already have both when the five-zone set is done at ~31 h and 87 % by 45 h; the median wait from the start of the lap is ~20 h, p90 ~50 h; **every Fuego Nuevo wipes the bag, so each lap needs the pair again and the number of finds required does not shrink with the Fuego Nuevo quantity bonus — if late laps feel capped by this, keep these two items through the reset (`FuegoNuevoRules.KeptItemTypes` and the delete SQL of `FuegoNuevoRepository.RenewAsync`)**); the non-Mythic part measures ~14 h of perfect play (the pacing script's 571 min counts one zone at a time — the drops must be farmed zone by zone: 5 × 167). **Since v0.14.1 `/forge-special` (`aa forgespecial`, `Modules/ForgeSpecialModule.cs`) shows those two recipes ALWAYS, from the first day, with the player's have/need (✅/❌) per ingredient and a "do not dismantle or sell these" warning**: they ask for the drops of all 5 zones, and players who did not know used `/dismantle` on materials they later needed. It is view-only on purpose (crafting stays in `/forge` once the gate is open: crafting the Trinche early would put +128 ATQ in the weapon slot) and draws with the same `ForgeModule.AddRecipes` as the blacksmith so both screens agree; it reads with `GetByDiscordIdAsync` (looking does not create an account). Before that they only appeared once the gate was open: in the forge menu/autocomplete (`RecipeCatalog.ViewFor(..., includeGate)`), on the LAST zone's recipes page in their own block, and in `/tips`, only once the gate is open.
**Winning is transactional**: `AdventureRepository.ApplyVictoryInternalAsync`, in the SAME transaction as the reward, sets `gate_cleared = true`, `in_gate = false` and sends the player back to the last normal zone (it does not touch `highest_zone_cleared`), returning `LevelUpOutcome.Gate = GateEvent.Cleared`; the first time a player beats the LAST zone's boss it returns `GateEvent.Opened` (the victory embed announces the gate). Events `gate_win`; achievement Asador (1/3/10, `Plain`).

**The Tabernero del Inframundo (v0.17.0; `/deepshop`, `aa deepshop|inframundo|deep`; `GameData/DeepShop.cs`, `Modules/DeepShopModule.cs`, `IDustRepository.BuyWithDustAsync`; NO migration)** — the owner (2026-10-14) after a lap with 5 Meteoritos and 0 Cortezas: the two Mythic gather items (Corteza del Árbol de Vida with `/chop`, Fragmento de Meteorito with `/mine`, 0.5 % per use each, a symmetric roll — checked in code and catalog, it is plain bad luck) are needed ONE each per lap (the Trinche and the Brasa) and the Fuego Nuevo wipes the bag, so getting both costs on average 25 h of non-stop `/chop`+`/mine` per lap (median 20 h, p90 49 h, p99 88 h) and never shrinks with the laps while the rest of the lap does. He rejected a pity/guarantee system; he proposed a vendor. **The vendor sells both for 3.000 Polvo each** (`DeepShop.PricePerUnit`, the one place to change it: "todo lo que farmeo me da para mucho más, luego vemos si lo dejamos más caro"; desmantelar a Mythic pays 500, so there is no dismantle-and-rebuy loop). It is a scene like the tavern: his line, your Polvo, the two items with price/how many you hold/what they are for, a select menu (ONE unit per pick, `deepshop_buy:{owner}`) and `/deepshop item:corteza|meteorito cantidad:1-10` (`DeepShop.MaxPerPurchase`) / `aa deepshop corteza 2` for more; the answer goes in a separate message and the scene refreshes. The purchase is ONE transaction with a guarded `UPDATE users SET dust = dust - @cost WHERE dust >= @cost` + `InventoryUpsert` (10 simultaneous buys with Polvo for one → exactly one). **He is known after beating the boss of the LAST zone** (`FogonRules.IsGateOpen`, the same rule that opens El Fogón) **and, once you have done a Fuego Nuevo, for ever** (my decision: the Fuego Nuevo closes the gate again but not the memory; remove the second condition in `DeepShop.IsOpen` to close him every lap); not requiring the Fogón gear is deliberate — that gear needs what he sells. Before that `/deepshop` only says nobody knows what you are talking about. Event `deepshop_buy`. His photo (since v0.18.5) is an APPLICATION EMOJI, `tabernero_deep` (`NpcImages.DeepShopkeeperEmoji`): the thumbnail is that emoji's CDN URL (`ItemDisplay.ImageUrl(..., 256)`), so nothing is hosted; `Images__DeepShopkeeper` (a URL) or a 256x256 `Assets/npc/deepshop.jpg` still win if present. Harness: `deepshoptest` (~45 checks, including the 10-way race, the routing of `deepshop_buy:{owner}` and the 100-character limit of the slash description, which the first draft broke — it would have stopped ALL command registration). **Ideas he liked but we did not build:** two Mythic guardian bosses in Zone 0 (guaranteed first-clear drop per lap; they need the Fogón entry rule changed and their own cooldown, because the global 5 h boss cooldown would add ≥ 10 h per lap) and the Mythic chance rising with the Fuego Nuevo.

**Fuego Nuevo, the voluntary reset (v0.12.0; `/fuegonuevo`, `aa fuegonuevo|fn|renacer`, `/blessings`, `aa bendiciones|blessings`; `Modules/FuegoNuevoModule.cs`, `Repositories/FuegoNuevoRepository.cs` + `BlessingRepository.cs`, `Services/PlayerBonusService.cs`, `GameData/FuegoNuevoRules.cs` + `BlessingCatalog.cs` + `PlayerBonuses.cs`, `Database/add_fuego_nuevo.sql` — run it on a live database BEFORE the v0.12 binary: every player query reads `users.fuego_nuevo`/`run_started_at` and every fight reads the blessing tables)** — the owner's "time travel" (design: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-06-fuego-nuevo-design.md`).
Needs `users.gate_cleared` (beat the Asador). The flow is two clicks, both with the owner's id in the custom id (the module has NO `[Group]`, so the ids reach it as written; routing checked with the real `InteractionService`): `/fuegonuevo` shows what is lost and what stays with real counts → `fn_start:{owner}:{count}` → class pick (picking IS the confirmation) `fn_go:{owner}:{count}:{class}` → `IFuegoNuevoRepository.RenewAsync`.
**ONE transaction**: lock the user row `FOR UPDATE`, guard `gate_cleared` AND `expectedCount` (a stale/double click gets `StaleCount`, never a second reset; 20 concurrent renewals → exactly one, tested), then reset via the columns' own `DEFAULT`s (level/XP/HP, so a reset player equals a `/start` one), weapon/amulet + enchants, Polvo, zone → the first normal zone by `min_level`, `highest_zone_cleared`, gate flags (`gate_cleared` goes back to false: every lap needs the Asador again),
`fuego_nuevo + 1`, `run_started_at`; deletes the inventory EXCEPT `items.type` `Huevo`/`PetFood` (`FuegoNuevoRules.KeptItemTypes` — **my call, the owner only said pets stay**: losing an unhatched egg or the food that levels the pets would punish exactly what he said is kept), and the banquete buff — **cooldowns are NOT touched since v0.46.0** (it used to delete every cooldown except `buybox`, which wiped the `vote` row and let the same top.gg vote be collected again after each Fuego Nuevo); writes the `fuego_nuevo_history` row and the 3-blessing offer (`blessing_offers`, kept until chosen, so closing Discord loses nothing) and pays the Alforja. **Stays**: gold, bank, daily streak, pets, achievements, mission claims, trophies, `player_stats`, blessings. Event `fuego_nuevo`; achievement Renacido (1/5/20, `Plain`). Refused while in a fight or raid (an in-memory check, NOT atomic with the transaction — accepted: the in-memory fight only touches the DB through HP deltas).
**The percentages are linear per lap and live in `FuegoNuevoRules`** (agreed with the owner on 2026-10-06): `/hunt` drop chance +30 % of the base per Fuego Nuevo (FN 10 = ×4: 6 % → 24 %), `/travel` drop chance +10 % for the first, 0.5 points less each lap, floor +2 % (base 40 % since v0.10.2; FN 10 = 71 %), `/chop` and `/mine` quantity +20 % (FN 10 = ×3), XP `(99 + x) × x / 2 %` since v0.28.0 (+50 % at FN 1, +545 % at FN 10; it was +10 % per lap, a step I had assumed). Gold and the boss chest do not move. Measured with `report_recipe_pacing.sql` (via `pacing_mult.sql`): the 5-zone recipe path drops to ~49 % of lap 1's time at FN 10 and ~34 % at FN 30.
**`PlayerBonuses` is the one object everything reads** (pets + FN + blessings; each source is a multiplier and they MULTIPLY): `CombatRewardCalculator.Roll*Reward(..., bonuses)`, `PlayerCombatProfileCalculator.Resolve(..., bonuses)` (attack/defense/HP of the blessings go in here so `/profile` shows the number that fights; the HP bonus is folded into `Passives.MaxHpMultiplier` so `ToDbHpDelta` still returns REAL HP), `GatheringModule.RollQuantityAsync` (`GatheringYield.RollScaled` rounds the decimals at random so the AVERAGE is exact — ×1.2 on a 1-unit item gives 2 one time in five), `/pet feed` (the Buen Pienso cooldown is threaded through `PetRules.CanEat/RemainingCooldown`, `PetRepository.FeedAsync(..., cooldown)` and the views; the database still keeps the clock). It is built by `IPlayerBonusService.GetAsync(discordId, fuegoNuevo)` — it **never throws** (a failed read logs and that part is zero; Manada scales the pets once, here). **Duels and the Arena call `Resolve` without bonuses on purpose**: nothing permanent counts against other players (the PvP balance in `PvpTuning` was measured without them).
**Blessings** (13 in `BlessingCatalog.All`; one per Fuego Nuevo, picked among 3 random non-maxed ones; repeating raises the level, I–V): speed (Mano de Leñador / Pico Fino +5 % quantity per level, Brasa Vieja / Ojo de Halcón +5 % relative drop chance, Aprendiz +4 % XP), power — SMALL on purpose and only against monsters (Filo Antiguo +1 % attack, Piel Curtida +1 % defense, Corazón de Brasa +2 % HP per level), pets (Manada +10 % to all pet bonuses, Buen Pienso −5 min of feeding wait), Bolsillo Hondo +3 % gold, Alforja del Fogonero (3 pet food + 1 Cajón de Pino per level, **when picked and again at every Fuego Nuevo** — not a delta), Brasa de Color (cosmetic: a title and a color for `/profile`). The planned "Buen Mate" (heal) was dropped from the pool to keep the surface small. None touches a cooldown (everything is calibrated per minute).
**`/class` is locked** since v0.12.0 (my call, matching the owner's "class locked per run"): `IUserRepository.TrySetClassWhileFreshAsync` is ONE guarded `UPDATE ... WHERE level <= 1 AND xp <= 0` (a stale button cannot change a playing character); the class is picked again inside `RenewAsync`; `/start` still uses `SetClassAsync`. Harness: `fntest` (141 checks, including the 20-way concurrency of renewing and choosing).

**The story: Las Crónicas del Fogón (v0.15.0; `/story`, `aa historia|story|cronicas|lore`; `GameData/Lore.cs`, `Modules/StoryModule.cs`; design, reasons and the full pitch: `docs/lore/cronicas-del-fogon.md`)** — the owner asked for "más historia de lo que pasa" and approved the draft ("me encanta así como está"); his direction: the Asador is a deep TRAGEDY, **each Fuego Nuevo frees him a bit of that feeling, 100 Fuegos Nuevos change the story for ever (left open for the future)**, new things appear from the second lap and the story "jumps" while you play like a game with a purpose. The purpose: **free the Asador**; the player is "the one who always comes back". Three acts, all text in `GameData/Lore.cs` (like `NpcDialogue`; a new chapter or scene is a row):
**Act I — Subir** (lap 1): the prologue and one chapter per ladder boss plus the Asador's, each opened the FIRST time the player beats that boss (`Lore.ChapterForBoss(monsterName)`; chapters are keyed by the boss NAME in the database, like the dialogue, never by zone id). **Act II — Soltar** (Fuegos Nuevos 1–99): the Asador's *pesar* is `100 − FN` % and the 40 plates of his long table go down 0.4 per lap (`Lore.Pesar/Plates`), and 13 scenes open at laps 1 (two), 2, 3, 5, 10, 15, 25, 35, 50, 75, 90 and 99 (the fire narrates them in the third person; the plates the prose names are CHECKED against `Lore.Plates` by the harness). **Act III** is the single scene at FN 100, "El que volvió" (ends with "Esto no termina acá": what changes in the game itself — a title, the name of the world, the Asador at the camp — is open on purpose). 100 laps are more than a thousand hours, so the emotional weight is in the first 25.
**Nothing is stored**: what a player can read comes from what already exists (`StoryProgress.For(user, orderedZones)`: the RANK of `highest_zone_cleared` in the ladder, `gate_cleared`, `fuego_nuevo`). With one Fuego Nuevo done the whole of Act I is open (the highest zone cleared goes back to 0 every lap, but the chapters stay). Sealed things show NOTHING, not even the title (`StoryModule.Compose` falls back to the index if a sealed key is requested by hand). **The story gives no power** (no stats, gold or drops): it touches no balance. **How it jumps** (all through the existing event → `ProgressNotifier` → notice queue → delivered when the command ENDS, private, one hour of life): a first win over a boss records the event `story_chapter` (`GameEventExtensions.RecordStoryChapterAsync`, called by the solo fight AND by every raid participant, only when `BossChest.IsFirstClear` and the player has no Fuego Nuevo yet) → "📖 Capítulo desbloqueado"; the `fuego_nuevo` event → "📖 Escena nueva del fuego" if that lap opens one (the counter IS the Fuego Nuevo number); the first `pet_hatched` and the first `craft` give one line from the Tabernero / the Herrero. The victory embed of a first boss clear shows that chapter's `FirstClearLine`; `/fuegonuevo` shows the pesar. From lap 1 the Herrero and the Tabernero greet differently (`NpcDialogue.Blacksmith/Shopkeeper(..., fuegoNuevo)`, only the greeting) and **the Asador's own lines change in the fight** at laps 10, 25, 50 and 90 (`NpcDialogue.AsadorStages`; `MonsterSays` passes `state.Bonuses.FuegoNuevo`). The achievements Compañero del Asador and El que volvió ride the same counter. Harness: `storytest` (88 checks, including the first clear with the real database, the routing of `story_pick:{owner}` with the real `InteractionService` and the box-opening list without rarity).
