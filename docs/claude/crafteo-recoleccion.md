# Forja, recetas, recolección, pesca, cocina, herramientas, Polvo, encantamientos y oficios

**Forge recipes (v0.27.0: 5 per zone + 1 without a zone; a player sees 2 per zone) — and the recipe book** (`recipes.zone_id` + `recipes.affinity`; `add_recipe_zone_and_affinity.sql` for old DBs). The per-zone template is **4 class weapons (one per class — the weapon family of that class, class-locked with `class_requirement`) + 1 amulet** (25 recipes), and there is ONE more recipe with **no zone** (`recipes.zone_id IS NULL`): the **Hoja de Acero Puro**, the only *general* weapon (+9 ATQ, no family, any class), made ONLY of gathered materials (2 Hierro + 10 Madera de Pino, ~48 min of `/mine`, 40 gold) so anyone can buy a base weapon without spending monster drops. **History, so nobody repeats it**: until v0.26.3 every zone had a general weapon (no family) next to the class weapon, which paid ×1.5 (`ClassWeaponSynergy`); the owner (2026-10-26): "nadie las hace, porque se ahorra los drops con el arma con sinergia o el amuleto" — the class weapon is always better and everyone only forges their own class's, so the synergy was always on and the general was a dead recipe. Now the ×1.5 is baked into the class weapon's number (ATQ 14/30/48/75/120 = the old 9/20/32/50/80 × 1.5 with the same rounding, `Database/remove_synergy_and_general_weapons.sql` for a live database BEFORE the v0.27.0 binary, otherwise every weapon would hit a third less), the four general weapons of zones 2–5 lost their recipe (the ITEMS stay in the catalog: whoever owns one keeps it and can sell it; `rework_drops_and_recipes.sql` calls them `retirada`), and sell prices were not touched. Combat, duels, the Arena, the team boss and the zone ladder do not move (same effective ATQ). The class weapon (2 gathered + the hunt drop of its route + the travel drop, ~150 min) and the *amulet* (1 gathered + ALL 3 drops of the zone, ~225 min) are unchanged. Max 4 ingredient types per recipe.
`affinity` is an explicit flag (set by the rework script) rather than inferred from the item. **What a player sees** lives in one pure place, `GameData/RecipeCatalog.cs`: `ViewFor` (the menu of `/forge` and its autocomplete: the current zone + the zone-less recipe + the Fogón gear when the gate is open; `ForgeChoices` hides the Hoja while a weapon is worn) and `BookFor` (the **recipe book**, see below); both filter by class (`IsVisibleTo`). **Since v0.28.1 this recipe book is the OPTIONAL "full" one (a `/config` choice); the DEFAULT is the classic zone-by-zone page described in the next paragraph.** **The recipe book (`/forge` button "📜 Ver todas las recetas", `aa forge recipes [zona]`, `ForgeModule.RenderRecipesEmbed`)** copies the look of EPIC RPG's `rpg recipes`: ALL zones in one message, one line per recipe — `<emoji> **Name** \`+14 ATQ\` → 12 <emoji> + 4 <emoji> … · 💰 100` (every item shows its own emoji, an ingredient without one its name) — grouped in one embed field per zone ("🌾 Zona 1 · Praderas del Mate · nivel 1", with 🔒 if the player's level is below the zone's), then "🧰 Sin zona específica" and, with the gate open, "🔥 El Fogón Eterno". No ✅/❌ and no "tenés X de Y": the pick menu already marks what you can forge. A Discord embed is capped at **6000 characters in total** (not just 1024 per field) and `EmbedBuilder.Build()` *throws* past it: the renderer stops at 5800 and says `aa forge recipes <zona>` shows the rest (with the tools and more zones planned it will need pages). Recipe seeds verify themselves (`seed_recipes.sql` raises if an item/zone is missing — otherwise the row is silently dropped or created without that ingredient). The same template holds for every zone (all of it in `rework_drops_and_recipes.sql`).
Deleting catalog items is dangerous: `inventory.item_id` is `ON DELETE CASCADE` (it silently wipes player inventories),
so any script that deletes items must first abort if anyone holds them (see `trim_recipes_to_zone_template.sql`) — except `rework_drops_and_recipes.sql`, which deletes on purpose (the owner approved it, no refund) and says what was lost with a NOTICE.

**The recipe book has TWO forms, chosen per player in `/config` (v0.28.1; `PlayerSettings.FullRecipeBook`, column `player_settings.full_recipe_book`, `Database/add_recipe_book_setting.sql` — run it on a live database BEFORE the v0.28.1 binary: the settings read asks for the column; harness `v0281test`)** — the owner (2026-10-27): "a pedido de los jugadores vamos a volver al menú de forge recipes previo… este está muy apretado, pide muchas cosas distintas y es más complicado de utilizar… por defecto quiero el que estaba antes, y el nuevo que se pueda cambiar en config", and "es el diseño viejo pero con lo actual; el general que no es de zona dejalo en la zona 1". **(1) CLASSIC, the default** (`ForgeModule.RenderZonePageEmbed`, `RecipeCatalog.PageFor`): the v0.26.3 design with today's recipes — ONE zone per page, one non-inline field per recipe (title = the item's emoji and name; under it `**+14 ATQ** · _arma de tu clase_`, the gold and each ingredient on its own line), and with the inventory known every ingredient says `✅/❌ … — have/need` and the gold `(tenés N)`. `/forge` shows the greeting + the forge list + a SECOND select menu `blacksmith_zone:{owner}` ("Ver las recetas de una zona", one option per zone with recipes) that swaps the scene to that zone's page; `aa forge recipes [zona]` prints the page of the player's current zone or the one asked for. What a player sees is only their class (their class weapon + the amulet), the Fogón gear goes on the LAST zone's page with the gate open, a zone above their level carries a 🔒 note. **The zoneless recipe (the Hoja de Acero Puro) shows ONLY on the first zone's page** (`PageFor` drops it elsewhere); the forge LIST (`ViewFor`, `ForgeChoices`) still offers it from any zone. **(2) FULL** (the v0.27.0 one: every zone in one message, one line per recipe, the "📜 Ver todas las recetas" button `blacksmith_book` / `blacksmith_home`): picked with the `/config` button "Recetario completo" or `aa config recetas completo` (`clasico` goes back). **Spacing (also v0.28.1)**: each part of the full book except the last ends with a line break + a zero-width space (`ForgeModule.SectionGap`, U+200B: Discord trims plain whitespace at the end of a field value but not this) so a zone separates visually from the next; `SectionLines` reserves those 2 characters inside the 1024 of the field. **Who decides**: `BlacksmithModule` (it takes `IPlayerSettingsService`) and `aa forge recipes` / `aa herrero` read `FullRecipeBook` per player; `BlacksmithModule.BuildSceneAsync(..., showBook, fullBook, viewZoneId)` is the one scene builder (the book/home buttons always build the full mode, since they only exist there, even if the player changed `/config` with an old message on screen); `ForgeModule.BuildRecipeBookForPlayerAsync` is the one function the text command calls. **A trap found while testing**: `string.EndsWith(string)` compares by CULTURE and ignores a zero-width space, so `"abc".EndsWith("\u200B")` is true — use `StringComparison.Ordinal` whenever a test or code looks for that character. Same `PlayerSettingsService` rule as the other option: after saving one option it RE-READS the whole row to refill the cache (building the cache entry from only the changed option would reset the other to its default when the cache was cold).

**Tools: the Carpenter and the axe/pickaxe (v0.29.0; `/tools`, `aa tools|herramientas|equipamiento|equipment|carpintero`; `GameData/ToolRules.cs`, `Modules/ToolsModule.cs`, `Repositories/ToolRepository.cs`, `Database/add_tools.sql` — run it on a live database BEFORE the v0.29.0 binary: `PlayerBonusService` reads `player_tools` on every gather and fight; harness `toolstest`)** — the owner (2026-10-27): "quiero que sea otro comando para no sobrecargar todo… un carpintero o constructor, que sea el de las herramientas, que solo con materiales se haga la herramienta… zona 3 y zona 5 por ahora; de base está la zona 1, que es lo actual ×1… que el menú de herramientas no esté en el perfil, que sea `aa equipamiento` o `aa herramientas`… así luego capaz podemos poner granjas o cook… 2 picos y 2 hachas". **Rules**: no tool = ×1 (today's rate); each kind has two tiers, the Zone 3 one (×2) and the Zone 5 one (×3), craftable once that zone is UNLOCKED (`ZoneRanking.MaxUnlockedRank`, the `/zona` rule); they multiply the quantity of `/chop` (axe) or `/mine` (pickaxe) for **Común, Raro and Épico only — Legendario and Mítico stay at 1** (`ToolRules.QuantityFactor`, applied per rolled rarity in `GatheringModule.ExecuteGatherAsync` and MULTIPLIED with Fuego Nuevo × blessing × profession × pet); a new tool REPLACES the old one and a worse one is refused, nothing spent; skipping a tier is allowed. **Data**: four `items` (type `Hacha` / `Pico`, `stat_value` = the multiplier, rarity Épico for Z3 and Mítico for Z5, no price), four `recipes` (`gold_cost` 0, `zone_id` = the zone that unlocks it, 4 gathered ingredients each, nothing else) and `player_tools(discord_id, kind axe|pickaxe, item_id)`.
