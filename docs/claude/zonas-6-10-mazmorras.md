Ranks of zones 1–5 do not change (they are ordered by `min_level` and the new ones are 30/35/40/45/50), so code that only needs the rank of a known zone (missions, Arena, world boss, raid multipliers) keeps the full list. Two SQL spots pick «the last normal zone» and now pick the player's top zone (`AdventureRepository.ApplyVictoryInternalAsync`: where the Asador's victory sends you, and whether a boss win is `GateEvent.Opened`). Several harnesses that took `zones[^1]` as «the last zone» now use `ForPlayer(…, 0)`. **(2) The zones**: each has ONE hunt monster with ONE simple drop (Cristal de Sal, Colmillo de Humo, Gota de Brea, Escama de Hielo, Pluma de Rayo; the normal hunt chance, `dust_value` 17), one travel monster that drops nothing (its `zone_boxes` `travel` row is the **Cofre de Escoria at 15 %**, what the Relic needs) and **no boss**; the other `zone_boxes` roles copy zone 5's. Gear: class weapons ATQ **180 / 270 / 400 / 600 / 900** and amulet DEF **165 / 215 / 280 / 365 / 475** (PvP rows in `PvpTuning`), named «Espada/Dagas/Arco/Grimorio/Amuleto de Sal|Humo|Brea|Hielo|Rayo»; recipes with lots of gathered material and more drops per zone (weapon drop ×8+2k, amulet ×10+2k, Hierro/Carbón/Nogal/Roble/Oro Puro/Piedra growing, and — the owner, 2026-11-02, at FN 7: «poné cantidades de mats grandes… el zafiro y el ébano salen bien» — Ébano/Zafiro ×4+2k (the only recipes where a Legendary goes above ×1; `healtest` exempts zones 6–10) plus, since v0.53.0, BOTH Mythic gather items (1 Fragmento de Meteorito + 1 Corteza del Árbol de Vida) in every class weapon and 1 + k of each in the amulet (k = 0 in Zone 6 … 4 in Zone 10), so those recipes have 6 ingredient types (until v0.52.2 only the weapons asked for ONE, the one of their material)). Monsters calibrated with the real resolver (`tools/balance2` part N): a common fight costs ~40 % of the life entering with the previous zone's gear at the minimum level and 5–10 % with the zone's own gear at min + 3, the monster lasting ~4 hits; the travel monster is the common ×1.25 HP / ×1.1 damage. `MissionRewards.GoldPerHunt` and `WorldBoss.WeaponLadder` have 10 values (560 / 840 / 1260 / 1890 / 2840 gold per hunt for zones 6–10). **(3) The Relic** (`items.type = 'Reliquia'`, one per zone, «Reliquia de Sal»…; recipe: the zone drop ×5+k, **1 Cofre de Escoria**, Madera de Pino and Piedra ×100+50k): forged like gear, goes to the bag (the boxes tab), cannot be sold (`sell_price` 0) or dismantled, does not survive the Fuego Nuevo, and is **spent when a dungeon run ENDS** (won, lost or timed out; a guarded decrement in `DungeonRepository.FinishAsync`; a run interrupted by a restart spends nothing). **(4) The dungeon** (`/boss` or `aa boss` standing in a dungeon zone opens it; with companions it is refused: «se hace solo»): `DungeonService.StartAsync` checks the zone, the boss cooldown, HP, the level of the NEXT zone (like a boss; the top zone asks none) and the Relic, then claims the boss cooldown with its 30-minute retry (6 simultaneous starts → one run); the run lives in memory (`DungeonSession`, a lock, a token in the button ids `dg:{owner}:{token}:{action}`, a 3-minute per-move clock that loses the run). **Each mistake takes a % of the max life** (`DungeonRules.MistakePercent`: 12 / 10 / 6 / 10 / 9), 0 life = lost; **no food inside** (`FightGuard.IsInFight` counts dungeons and `FightLock.IsAllowedInDungeon` only lets through `aa dg`, profile, inventory, cd, info, tutorial — not `/use`). The five puzzles are pure (`GameData/Dungeons`, each `IDungeonPuzzle`, drawn per run): **Zone 6 «El patrón del fuego»** (Simon-like: a series of 🔥💧🪨 to memorize and answer with counters — 💧 apaga 🔥, 🪨 frena 💧, 🔥 derrite 🪨 —, 4 floors of 3–6, each floor the previous series + one), **Zone 7 «El laberinto de humo»** (a perfect maze carved by DFS, 5 wide × 8 tall, braided (a DFS tree plus 6 opened walls, so there are several ways to the exit), with the walls drawn between the cells, fog, free arrows, 6 hidden embers on those ways (there is always one clean route), NO step limit — **reworked in v0.52.0 and v0.52.2, see the two paragraphs right after this one**; in the TOP zone it sends you **straight against the Asador** (`AdventureCombatStarter.PrepareAsadorFromDungeonAsync`: no cooldown claim, `BossZoneId` 0 so its victory is the usual gate win, `CombatState.FromDungeon`, **`HealUsed` = true so no food**, starts with the life the dungeon left even if the player ate something meanwhile through the tavern buttons, stats from `AsadorScaling` per top zone and the **Flare**: `MonsterSpecialChancePercent` 20, the hit ×1.75 that turn, its own line in the fight). The fight is a new message whose session points to it (`MessageCombatReplyTarget`). Asador stats per top zone (HP / damage): 2286–2794 / 223–273, 3186–3894 / 275–336, 4446–5434 / 337–411, 6336–7744 / 410–502, 9126–11154 / 510–624 — ~30 % defeat with the top zone's gear at min + 3 arriving at 80 % life, ~17 % at 100 %, ~47 % at 60 %. **Since v0.48.1 the life of that table is multiplied by `AsadorScaling.HpMultiplier` = 1.5** (damage untouched; `BaseStatsFor` is the calibrated table, `StatsFor` what is fought; no migration): the owner, playing at FN 7, beat the Zone 6 Asador without any Zone 6 gear, because the table was calibrated for a player at min level + 3 and a much higher level makes the previous zone's gear enough (Zone 5 gear at level 60: 2 % defeat arriving at 100 %). Measured with `dotnet run --project tools/balance2 -- N hp` (4 classes, ability, no food), top zone 6 arriving at 80 %: previous zone's gear 32 % → 72 % defeat at level 50 and 10 % → 36 % at level 60; the zone's own gear 2 % → 11 % and 0 % → 2 %; a player arriving exactly at min + 3 with the zone's gear goes from ~30 % to ~52–64 %. ×2 was rejected (own gear at min + 20: 29–44 % defeat, too harsh for a dungeon that costs a Relic); the Zone 5 boss was NOT touched (its raid is tuned with its stored life ÷ 1.25, move both together) and the real lever, scaling a boss with the player's level above the zone's, is still unbuilt. **(6) The old gate**: from FN 5 `/zona 0` refuses («se llega pasando la mazmorra») and a player left standing in the gate who tries `/boss` is taken out of it (`CombatStartStatus.GateViaDungeon`, no cooldown spent); the Trinche and the Brasa are not required any more from zone 6 (solved in v0.53.0: from Fuego Nuevo 5 the forge no longer offers them, `/forge-special` says they are not needed and forging them by name is refused, see the v0.53.0 second-half paragraph). **The smoke maze, free, with the walls drawn and embers that bite (v0.52.0; `GameData/Dungeons/SmokeMaze.cs`, harness `v0470test` section «laberinto»; NO migration)** (**the size, the ember placement and the burn text of this paragraph were replaced in v0.52.2: see the next one; the free arrows, the drawn walls, the vision and the lack of limits stay**) — the owner (2026-11-09, playing Zone 7): «no te deja moverte en dirección libre, te guía básicamente… quiero que sea libre y que haya más trampas, solo con 1 me tropecé», then, on mock-ups I generated with the real algorithm: «cada pared se vea si no me deja moverme… me gusta que se vea el recorrido… que se vean los cuadrados que vienen: si tenés 2 caminos en blanco pero pared que se vea en negro, si tenés llama es blanco, obviamente una vez te quemás es por un camino que no va a la salida» and «sin pasos límite… no está mal que sean varios pasos»; and after my first build: «saquemos el aviso del calor, o agrandemos el laberinto, o que haya más bifurcaciones con 1 trampa, de última más de 6 trampas… no es la idea hacerlos perder pero si no se comen las trampas no tiene sentido». **Why it felt guided**: the arrows against a wall came out disabled, so in a corridor only ONE arrow was lit, the map showed the open cells beside you, and 3 embers in 25 cells (all off the shortest path, i.e. only inside dead-end branches) were almost never touched. **Lesson of the first build of this version** (5×5, 6 loose embers off the path, a heat line, shortest path ≤ 17): measured with a player who picks at random among the white squares and turns back when burned, it cost **1.03 burns per run, the same as before** — adding embers anywhere off the path does not make them bite, because a side branch is entered only on a wrong turn. What makes them bite is WHERE they are. **Now**: (1) the 4 arrows are ALWAYS enabled; pressing one against a wall (or the outer edge) moves nothing, costs no step and no life, and answers «🧱 Ahí hay una pared.» — it returns `DungeonStep.Ok` with a `Note`, NOT `Ignored`, because the service/module do not re-render on `Ignored` (the note would never show). (2) `Render()` draws a grid of emojis, 11 wide × 15 tall (`Cols` 5 × `Rows` 7 cells; cells on the odd rows/columns, the step or wall between two cells on the even ones, corners and outer border always ⬛): from the cell you stand on you SEE its 4 sides — open ⬜ (and the cell behind it) or wall ⬛ — and what you saw stays drawn (`_known` edges, `_seen` cells); the rest is 🌫️; what you walked is 🟩 (a step between two walked cells, which in a tree maze is always a step you took), you are 🧍, a burned ember 🔥, the exit 🚪 as soon as it is one of the squares you see. The maze grows DOWN because the WIDTH is what can wrap on a phone (11 emojis fit, 13 may not; not tested on a real phone). (3) **The embers sit at the MOUTH of every branch that leaves the shortest path** (`mouths` in the constructor: cells off the route that touch it through an open step; at most `MaxTraps` = 6, chosen at random if there are more) and look exactly like any other white square until stepped on; stepping burns ONCE (`DungeonStep.Mistake`, 10 % of the max life as before: `DungeonRules.MistakePercent`), leaves the cell 🔥 and says «Ese camino no lleva a la salida: volvé», which is TRUE by construction (an ember is never on the shortest path, and in a perfect maze that path is the only one). So every wrong turn at a fork costs 10 %, and a burn tells you that the other white square is the way. (4) The maze is re-rolled until it has at least `MinBranches` = 4 such branches (about 1 of 4 carved mazes qualifies; guard of 500). With the 5×7 size the route is ~26 steps and each maze has ~4.2 embers. (5) **No heat line** (the first build had «🌡️ Sentís calor»): with the embers at the branch mouths it would light at almost every fork and say nothing useful, and it was the opposite of «libre». **No step limit**: `StepLimit`/`StepSlack` and the «el humo te ahoga» mistake are gone; `Steps` is only shown («Pasos: N»). The only things that cost life are the embers (at most 60 %) and the 3-minute per-move clock still ends the run. **Balance, measured** (`tools/` is not involved: a node simulation of the same algorithm and the guard inside `v0470test`, 400 mazes, a player who picks at random among the white squares and turns back when burned): **2.19 burns per run (~22 % of the life), none in 5 % of the runs, 6 or more in 0.0 %, ~30 steps**; a player who heads toward the far corner (the exit is the farthest cell from the entrance, so that is a fair guess) burns ~1.7-2.1. The skill left is that guess, not information: at a fork there is nothing on the screen that tells the two white squares apart. The guard in `v0470test` fails if the mean leaves 1.7-2.6 burns, if 6+ reaches 1.5 %, or if the steps leave 22-40 — retune `Rows`, `MinBranches`, `MaxTraps` or `MistakePercent(SmokeMaze)` together (in the node simulation 5×6 with 4 branches gave ~2.1 burns and ~26 steps, and 5×8 with 5 branches ~2.6 and ~35). Not balanced against the Asador fight that follows in Zone 10 (the life you arrive with is whatever the embers left). English strings were added (`Locales/en.json`, `tools/loc --check` 0/0/0/0). The typed `aa dg arriba|abajo|izquierda|derecha` is unchanged. The **other dungeons were NOT touched** (the owner only asked about Zone 7).

**The smoke maze, braided, with six embers (v0.52.2; `SmokeMaze.Loops` / `Traps` / `Slack` / `MinShortest`, harness `v0470test` section «laberinto»; NO migration)** — the owner (2026-11-10), after playing it: «es muy obvio los callejones sin salida y el camino a tomar, no hay duda de cuál elegir… estaría bueno tener más bifurcaciones, dos caminos que lleguen a la salida igual pero por uno tenés una llama… ayer no me comí ninguna» and, on my proposal (5×8, 6 loops, 3 embers): «poné 6 brasas con ese recorrido… dejá 6 brasas». **Why v0.52.0 was too easy**: in a perfect maze (a tree) there is ONE route, and the embers at the mouth of the side branches let a player who saw a branch end in a wall simply not enter it. **Now**: the DFS tree is carved and then `Loops` = 6 random interior walls are opened (each closes a cycle: 39 + 6 = 45 open steps in 40 cells), 5 wide × **8** tall; the exit is still the farthest cell and the draw is repeated until the short route is at least `MinShortest` = 20 (it measures ~22; about 8 draws per maze). The **`Traps` = 6 embers sit on cells of those routes** (cells whose entrance→cell→exit length is at most `Slack` = 6 above the short route; never the entrance or the exit) and a draw is accepted only if, with ALL the embers placed, the exit can still be reached avoiding them (`PlaceTraps`: add one, BFS, drop it if it cuts the last clean route): there is ALWAYS a clean route, but nothing on the screen says which. An ember still burns ONCE (10 % of the max life, `DungeonRules.MistakePercent`) and the cell stays 🔥 and can be crossed afterwards; the note no longer says «esa rama no sale» (false with loops): «🔥 ¡Una brasa! Te quemaste: esa casilla queda marcada y ya no quema.» A set of walked EDGES (`_walked`) replaced the tree assumption in `Tile`: with loops two visited cells may be joined by a step you did not take, which stays ⬜; only the steps you took are 🟩. **Balance, measured** (the harness explorer: depth-first, picks at random among the white squares it has not visited and backs up when stuck, 400 mazes): **4.4 burns per run on average (~44 % of the life), none in 1 % of the runs, all six in 31 %**, ~38 steps; the six embers cannot kill by themselves (6 × 10 % = 60 %), the dungeon is lost only by the 3-minute clock. A player who aims at the far corner burns ~3.5. Fewer dead ends (1.9 against 4.2 for a tree of the same size) and in 100 % of the mazes some step of the short route can be bypassed. With 3 embers (the first proposal, before he asked for 6) the random explorer burned ~2.1 (6 % of the runs none). **If it turns out too harsh the levers are, in order, `MistakePercent(SmokeMaze)` (7 % would put the average near 30 %), `Traps` and `Slack`**; the guard in `v0470test` fails if the mean leaves 3.6-4.8 burns, if all six reach 40 % of the runs, if the runs with none reach 6 % or if the steps leave 30-46; a mutation check with `Loops = 0` makes six checks fail. English strings changed (`tools/loc --check` 0/0/0/0). The other dungeons were NOT touched.
