# Balance: zonas, drops, cajas por zona, XP y jefes

> Texto movido literal desde el CLAUDE.md viejo el 2026-11-12 (en inglés, como estaba). El índice está en CLAUDE.md.

**Zones**: the world is split into difficulty-scaled zones (`zones` table, `Repositories/IZoneRepository.cs`).
Zone IDs are shown to players ("Zona 2"), so they should be consecutive 1..N in difficulty order; a failed
insert burns identity numbers and leaves gaps — `Database/renumber_zones_consecutively.sql` fixes an existing DB
(it rewrites every column that stores a zone id, so any *new* column holding a zone id must be added to it).
Each player has `users.current_zone_id` (default 1); `/zona [id]` moves them after validating
`zones.min_level`, `/zonas` lists them. `/hunt`, `/travel` and `/boss` are all zone-scoped, and every
monster lives in the DB (`monsters` + `monster_drops`, `Repositories/IMonsterRepository.cs`; nothing is
hardcoded in `MonsterCatalog` anymore). A zone has three *mutually exclusive* kinds of monster
(`monsters.is_boss` / `monsters.is_travel`, `CHECK (NOT (is_boss AND is_travel))`): the `/hunt` pool
(`PrepareHuntAsync`, **2 in every zone**), the boss (`/boss`, alone or with friends) and **one dedicated
`/travel` monster per zone** (`PrepareTravelAsync`, `Database/seed_travel_monsters.sql`). The travel monster
is the average of that zone's commons at HP ×1.25 / damage ×1.1 (an élite, not a boss — measured with the
real `CombatTurnResolver`, see the seed header), and `CombatRewardCalculator.RollTravelReward` pays the
**whole `/hunt` formula ×30** (`TravelRewardMultiplier` — what the 30-minute cooldown is worth in hunts; it was ×10 with a 10-minute cooldown until v0.7.1, so the payout per minute is unchanged). So
travel rewards follow the zone ladder automatically: a new zone's travel payout is set by the
`gold_reward`/`xp_reward` bonus of its monster, same as hunts. If you add a zone, add its travel monster or
`/travel` answers "zona sin monstruos" (without charging the cooldown). **A boss pays more than a travel**: `RollBossReward` is the `/hunt` formula (plus the boss's own big gold/XP bonus,
`seed_zone_bosses.sql`) ×15 (`BossRewardMultiplier`), i.e. 2.0–4.7× a travel of its zone (zone 1 the most; measured in the stage-11 harness at the zone's level). History: ×1 until v0.6.0 (it paid HALF a travel), ×6 for a 30-minute cooldown, and ×15 since v0.8.0
for a ONE-HOUR cooldown (parity per minute against the 30-minute travel would be ×12, the extra 25% was the owner's "un poco más"). Since v0.8.1 the boss cooldown is **5 hours** again (too many boss chests at 1 hour) and the owner asked NOT to touch rewards, so the ×15 stays and the boss now pays well under a travel per minute of cooldown — if it should feel worth the wait, raise this one constant; every raid participant gets the full amount. It speeds up
leveling (a player cycling hunt + travel + boss on cooldown levels faster than with ×1), so if pacing needs to slow down, lower this one constant. **The boss no longer drops a material: its single `monster_drops` row is the zone's CHEST** (`Cajón de Pino` Z1, `Baúl de Roble` Z2, `Arcón de Hierro` Z3, `Cofre de Oro` Z4, **`Cofre de Escoria` Z5 since v0.14.0**, and `Brasero del Fogón` for the Asador), 100%
the first time THAT player beats it **in this lap** (`BossChest.IsFirstClear`: `highest_zone_cleared` for the ladder, `gate_cleared` for the Fogón — both read before applying the victory, solo and per participant in a raid; both reset with each Fuego Nuevo) and, **since v0.14.0, repeats pay the smaller box of the zone's `zone_boxes` row with role `repeat` and ITS chance** (Z1 Pino 100 %, Z2 Pino, Z3 Roble, Z4 Arcón de Hierro 60 %, Z5 Arcón, Fogón Arcón; it used to be "40 % of the big chest" for every zone, which made the Cofre de Oro the most profitable thing in the game).
The chance comes from the database (`BossChest.RepeatAsync`, `CombatRewardCalculator.BossChestChancePercent(firstClear, repeatChancePercent)`; there is no `BossChestRepeatPercent` constant any more) and `/drops` prints the same row. The chest and the repeat box travel the
normal drop path (`ApplyBossVictoryAsync`) and are opened with `/open`; the Arca del Soberano stays achievement-only.

**Free boxes per zone: the `zone_boxes` table (v0.14.0, "cofres parejos"; `GameData/ZoneBoxes.cs`, `Repositories/ZoneBoxQueries.cs`, `Services/ZoneBoxService.cs`, `Database/seed_zone_boxes.sql` + `add_zone_boxes.sql`, design: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-07-cofres-parejos-design.md`)** — `zone_boxes(zone_id, role, box_item_id, chance_percent)` is the ONE place that says which free box a zone gives and when: roles `repeat` (a repeated boss win, with its chance), `daily` / `weekly` (completing all the missions of the day / week), `prize` (achievement tier II and the Arena champion) and `prize_top` (tier III). It replaced the box ladder that was hard-coded in `MissionRewards`, so a new zone needs rows, not code. `BoxGrant` (ZoneTier→prize, ZoneTierPlusOne→prize_top, Daily, Weekly) still says WHICH role a reward pays; `ZoneBoxTable` (pure) resolves it by the zone's rank and clamps an out-of-range rank (a missing role for a zone is an error and the payout throws: nothing is paid without its box). `RewardPayer` reads the table INSIDE the reward transaction; everything else goes through the 5-minute-cached `IZoneBoxService`, and the startup logs what is missing (`EnsureDatabaseAsync`). **The Cofre de Oro was cut from the free supply of the low zones** (it now shows up only from Zone 3's `prize_top`), because a free Cofre de Oro (~211k "farming minutes") outweighed several 2-hour sessions of play (measured by `Database/report_box_economy.sql`: Pino ~0.75k, Roble ~8.2k, Arcón ~43k, Cofre ~211k; a perfect 2 h session pays 3.4k / 13.9k / 28.3k / 51.6k / 90k of gold in Zones 1–5). `Database/report_economy.sql` prints the table and how many sessions a week of free boxes is worth per zone (3.5–9.9 in v0.14.0: still uneven between zones, Zone 4 being the most generous; it is data, tune it with an `UPDATE` on `zone_boxes`, no redeploy needed beyond the 5-minute cache). **Two new prize-only boxes** (not bought, not resold, `buy_price = 0`): **Cofre de Escoria** (Zone 5 boss chest, 35–65 items, Mythic rarity, gold bonus 3 % × 20–60k; its loot weights are in `seed_zone_boxes.sql`) and **Brasero del Fogón** (the Asador's chest, 25–45 items, 65 % of the draws are pet food, 40–80k gold ALWAYS: the Fuego Nuevo wipes materials, boxes and XP, so the Zone-0 prize has to be what stays — gold, pet food, pets). The Mythic fixed materials (Corteza / Meteorito) are still only in the Arca (`seed_zone_boxes.sql` verifies it).

**The /travel stopped dropping a material (v0.36.0; `Database/remove_travel_drops.sql` — run it on a live database BEFORE the v0.36.0 binary: the code reads the `travel` role of `zone_boxes` on every won /travel and the script deletes the 5 travel-drop items; `Services/TravelBox.cs`, `ZoneBoxRole.Travel`, `CombatRewardCalculator.TravelBoxChance` / `RollTravelReward(..., boxChancePercent)`, `CombatState.TravelZoneId`, harness `v0360test`)** — the owner (2026-10-28), thinking about "too many items in the inventory and too many ingredients per recipe" compared with EPIC: "¿y si sacamos el drop solo del travel y que el travel sea esa suma de xp y gold con probabilidad de sacar una caja de la zona?" and, on my proposal of bigger hunt-drop quantities, "sumale algún drop a cada uno". **Now**: a travel pays the whole hunt formula ×30 (gold and XP, unchanged) plus a CHANCE of the zone's small box, a new `zone_boxes` role `travel`: **20 % in every zone** (Z1 and Z2 Cajón de Pino, Z3 Baúl de Roble, Z4 and Z5 Arcón de Hierro: the zone's repeat box; my first proposal was 8 / 15 / 6 / 2 / 3 % — about 10-15 % of the travel's gold in farming-minute value — and the owner found it too little: «ponele 20 % a cada zona. olvidate»). One box every ~5 travels weighs a lot in Z4-Z5, where the box is an Arcón (~43k gold-equivalent): by my estimate (4 travels per perfect 2 h × 20 % × the box's value) it adds about +40-65 % on top of the gold of such a session there and +4-25 % in Zones 1-3; if it turns out too much, lower those rows with an `UPDATE` (5-minute cache; `ZoneBoxRole.All` and `Missing()` now ask for six roles per normal zone, the gate only the repeat). The chance is multiplied by `PlayerBonuses.TravelDropMultiplier` (Fuego Nuevo, pets, the Brasa Vieja blessing and the dragon's drop perk now act on the BOX chance; their texts say "caja") and capped at 100. `CombatState.TravelZoneId` (set by the starter at the start of a travel) says which zone's row to read; a travel started before the binary changed carries none and just pays no box; `TravelBox.ChanceAsync` never throws (a failure means no box, logged) and the victory shows "🎁 ¡Cayó una caja!" (`/open` it). **Data**: the 5 travel monsters stay but drop nothing; the 5 items (Cuero Grueso, Ceniza Bendita, Escoria Metálica Densa, Aliento de Fuego Eterno, Ceniza del Abismo) are deleted — whoever held some got **1 of EACH hunt drop of that zone per unit** (two ~33-minute units for one ~75-minute unit); the 25 class-weapon and amulet recipes of Zones 1-5 lost their travel ingredient (**4 → 3 types of ingredient**, the owner's complaint about recipes) and ask for more HUNT drops so the time stays about the same: Z1 weapons just lose the leather (gathering gates them at 95-100 min); **the amulets ended at ×4 of EACH of the two hunt drops in v0.38.0** (`remove_travel_drops.sql` first raised them to ×7 in Zones 2-5 and ×5 in Zone 1: see the v0.38.0 paragraph below); **the Z2-5 class weapons stay at ×4 of their route's drop** (`remove_travel_drops.sql` raised them to ×5 and the owner, the same day, asked «baja 1 todas las armas del tema drop»: `Database/rebalance_weapon_drops.sql`, a data-only script that a live database which already ran the first one runs on its own, and that `run_fresh_install.sql` runs right after it; production ran it on 2026-10-28 after the v0.36.0 release). Measured with `report_recipe_pacing.sql` (the model: 0.03 per minute per hunt drop): weapons ~133 min of drops, and the gathering of 6 Hierro floors them at 143 (so a weapon takes 143 min, like the 150 it took with the travel drop), amulets ~133 of drops since v0.38.0 (they were ~233 with ×7 and ~225 before the change), floored by gathering at 143-190. The Fogón gear (5 of the 10 hunt drops ×5 per piece) was not touched. The 10 remaining drops are 2 per zone, and the 3→1 exchange keeps working between each pair. Dust: the 38-per-unit travel value is gone (`Dismantling.TravelDropDust` removed). Box loot pools derive from the monsters' drops, so every box lost the travel drop (weight 2) and is worth ~7-8 % less in farming minutes (`report_box_economy.sql`, price over value: Pino 298 → 320 %, Roble 165 → 179, Arcón 94 → 102, Cofre de Oro 55 → 60); prices were not touched. `/drops` prints, for the travel block, "N% de caja" and the box instead of a drop. The migration is re-runnable (no travel drops = nothing to do) and sits after `add_dragon_food.sql` in `run_fresh_install.sql`; the scratch fresh install matched the live catalog row for row. **Everything below about the travel drop (the ~75 min per unit "binding resource", `TravelDropChancePercent` 40 %, dust 38, 15 drops, "ONE travel unit off each class weapon") describes the game up to v0.35.0.** Do NOT re-run `rework_drops_and_recipes.sql`, `rebalance_dust.sql` or `rebalance_crafting.sql` alone on a live database after this: they bring the travel drops back or fail because they no longer exist. `tools/balance2` still simulates travel drops (its `Cfg.Travel` is 0 now). Not decided yet: one drop per zone instead of two (it would lose the physical/arcane class route).

**Drops: every monster drops exactly ONE item, and the chances are deliberately low.** Per zone there are **3 drop materials** — one per `/hunt` monster (the two "a granel": the *physical* one that Guerrero and Ninja need and the *arcane*
one for Arquero and Hechicero) and one from the travel monster ("escaso", needed by every class weapon and by the amulet) — plus the boss's chest. 20 monsters in total (2 hunt + travel + boss in each of the 5 zones, `Perro Cimarrón` was
removed). `Database/rework_drops_and_recipes.sql` (v0.7.0) is the single source of truth for the roster, the boss chests and the recipes: it verifies the final state (exactly 2 hunt + 1 travel + 1 boss per zone, one drop each — a Material for
hunt/travel, a Caja for the boss). The chances live in `CombatRewardCalculator` and nowhere else: `HuntDropChancePercent` 6 (it was 10 until v0.6.0: lowered so that reaching a zone's level is not enough to breeze through it — you have to stay and farm the gear),
`TravelDropChancePercent` **40 since v0.10.2** (the owner: "60 es una banda"; it was 60 — with the 30-minute cooldown that was 0.02 drops per minute, the same as the old 20% per 10 minutes, which is what the recipes were calibrated against — so the recipe quantities were NOT retuned on purpose: the travel drops, the "escasos" of every class weapon and amulet, now come 1.5× slower and the 5-zone class-weapon + amulet path goes from ~41 h to ~52 h of perfect play, `report_recipe_pacing.sql`; the box economy barely moves, `report_box_economy.sql` Cofre de Oro 60 % → 57 % of its price) and the boss chest chances (the boss has its own constants on purpose: it used to share the hunt formula, so lowering hunt would have silently changed it). `/drops` (`aa drops`,
`Modules/DropsModule.cs` + the pure `GameData/DropsCatalog.cs`) lists every zone's monsters and drops from those same
constants, so the list can't drift. These are **run-1 baseline values** — the reset unlocked after Zone 5 is meant to
raise drop % and material quantity. **Recipe quantities are calibrated against these chances**, so changing a chance,
a monster or a drop means re-running `Database/report_recipe_pacing.sql` (minutes of farming per recipe; pass the
chances as `-v ph= -v pt=`; `pb` no longer matters) and retuning the recipes in the rework script. Note the trap this avoids: with one item per monster each
specific item drops *more* often than with two, so lowering the percentages alone would have made progress faster,
not slower. Targets in continuous-play minutes (the pacing script's model): general weapon (gone since v0.27.0 except the gather-only Hoja de Acero Puro, ~48 min of `/mine`; it was ~100 min until v0.14.1 and ~67 min with 2 of each ingredient from v0.14.2), class weapon ~150 designed (Zone 1 ~100; it was ~225 measured from v0.10.2, when the travel drop went to 40 % and nobody retuned the recipes, **and ~150 again since v0.15.1**), amulet ~200 designed (Zone 1 ~130; ~300 measured until v0.15.1, **~225 since**). **The binding resource of every Zone 2–5 recipe is the `/travel` drop** (40 % every 30 minutes = 75 min per unit; the hunt drops come 2–3× faster): that is why v0.15.1 took ONE travel unit off each class weapon (3 → 2) and each amulet (4 → 3) (`Database/rebalance_crafting.sql` for a live database; `rework_drops_and_recipes.sql` carries it for fresh installs), and why the next lever, if the path must get faster, is `TravelDropChancePercent` (50 % ≈ −6 h on the five-zone set) and not more recipe edits. **The Zone 5 class weapons stopped asking for a Mythic GATHER item in v0.15.1** (Fragmento de Meteorito / Corteza del Árbol de Vida, 0.5 % per action ≈ 1000 min and a tail of ~40 h): the mineral ones ask for Gema de Zafiro ×1 and the wooden ones for Madera de Nogal ×3 (like Zone 4); the only 0.5 % ingredients left are ONE Corteza (Trinche) and ONE Meteorito (Brasa) in the Fogón gear (see El Fogón Eterno), which are found along the whole lap in parallel with everything else. Measured with the full-journey Monte Carlo (scratchpad `balance2`, 160 runs per scenario, the real combat/drops/gathering/boss cooldown): the five-zone class set (weapon + amulet of every zone) went from **~40 h [p90 48 h] to ~31 h [p90 35 h]** of perfect play; skipping the amulets of Zones 1–4 ~22 h. Hard floor: the 5 h boss cooldown is GLOBAL (one cooldown for all zones), so beating the Zone 5 boss takes ≥ 20.8 h whatever the gear.

**Amulets and defense (v0.15.1; `Database/rebalance_amulets.sql`)** — defense is subtracted from each monster hit 1 to 1 (`CombatMath.ResolveMonsterHit`: damage = hit − defense, minimum 1, then the Warrior's ×0.9 and the dodge; the player's defense is the LEVEL plus the amulet), so the amulet felt weak: with the zone's amulet (18/30/46/75) a common fight still cost 11–20 % of the HP. The owner (2026-10-09): "cuando lo tengas deberías poder hacer autohunt sin perder vida casi". Now the amulets of Zones 2–5 are **35/60/85/120** (Zone 1 stays 10: it already cost ~1 %) and the Brasa del Fogón is **165**: a common fight with the zone's amulet costs **~3 %** of the HP and a `/travel` élite 4–6 % (measured with the real resolver, 4 classes × levels min+3/6/10, basic attack as `/autohunt`). Because the subtraction is flat, **each boss raised its damage by the same amount as its zone's amulet** (Lobisón Alfa +17, Capataz de Hierro +30, Señor del Volcán +39, Soberano de la Escoria +45, El Asador Eterno +45; `seed_zone_bosses.sql`, `seed_fogon.sql`): the boss exam with the zone's own gear is exactly what it was (2–13 % defeat) and with the previous zone's gear it is harder (~50–84 %, which is what the design said: 67–87 %). Hunt monsters and élites were NOT touched on purpose. Selling prices of the four amulets follow the stat (6 per point, capped at 60 % of the recipe gold, like every ladder piece). **PvP keeps the OLD amulet ladder**: `PvpTuning.Amulet` (used by `DuelFighterFactory`) swaps in the old DEF by amulet name, because the duel balance (`PvpTuning` HP factors) was measured with it and the new values would triple the duel length. **Raids were not re-measured**: the raid boss is the stored boss ×1.1 damage, so with +Δ on both sides it hits ~0.1·Δ (3–4) harder per blow. If a new zone or amulet is added, give the amulet its row in `PvpTuning.AmuletDefenseInPvp` or it will use its PvE stat in duels.

**Class balance against monsters: `GameData/PveTuning.cs` (v0.15.0)** — the mirror of `PvpTuning`: a combat-HP factor per class that applies ONLY against monsters (Ninja ×0.95, Archer ×1.15; Warrior and Mage 1.0). The owner asked "¿el Ninja está muy OP?" (he was in Zone 3 with a Zone 1 weapon and no amulet); measured with the real `CombatTurnResolver` and the live monsters (ability as soon as ready, no food, no fleeing): the Ninja forgives a missing gear better than any class (Zone 2 boss, Zone 1 weapon, no amulet, level 13: it loses 35 % of attempts against 51 % Warrior, 68 % Mage, 87 % Archer) but with the ladder's gear the Warrior is better (Zone 3 boss, own gear, level 16: Warrior 1 %, Mage 9 %, Ninja 6 %, Archer 20 %), and the Archer is the weak class in PvE exactly as it was in PvP. Skipping gear by over-levelling works for every class (Zone 3 commons with Zone 1 gear at level 16: ~0 % defeat, 40–56 % of the HP), the Ninja just needs 2–3 fewer levels; the Zone 3 boss with Zone 1 gear is still ~67 % defeat for the Ninja at level 19 and ≥92 % for the rest. Values chosen: Ninja ×0.90 would match the Warrior without gear but dropped it below the Mage with gear, so ×0.95; the owner asked NOT to give the Archer ×1.25 ("depende cómo lo juegues, el crítico pega mucho") and offered 1.15 or 1.20: ×1.15 leaves it level with the Mage with its own gear (Zone 3 boss level 16: 8 % vs 9 %) and still the most fragile without gear. It enters through `PlayerCombatProfileCalculator.Resolve` **only when `bonuses` is not null** (the monster fights pass them, `DuelFighterFactory` does not, so duels and the Arena keep the balance of `PvpTuning`) and is folded into `Passives.MaxHpMultiplier` like the Corazón de Brasa blessing, so `CombatState.ToDbHpDelta` still returns REAL HP. **Re-measure whenever a class passive, ability or monster changes** (the sim is a double loop over `CombatTurnResolver.ResolveTurn`). **The HP a player SEES is the combat one everywhere (v0.25.3; `GameData/HpDisplay.cs`, `PlayerCombatProfileCalculator.HpMultiplier`, `PlayerBonusExtensions.HpMultiplierAsync`, harness `v0253test`)** — the owner (2026-10-23, from a friend's screenshots where a Ninja's max HP went 323 → 340 inside ONE fight): "necesito que siempre sea la misma vida… sea la clase que sea mostrá la vida que debería tener: si es ×0.95, mostrá el resultado". Until v0.25.2 the fight showed the scaled HP (real × class factor × Vida enchant × Corazón de Brasa) while the victory, the profile, `/heal`, `/use` and the new-character screen showed the REAL one stored in the database, so the maximum "changed" between screens (340 × 0.95 = 323 and 257 × 0.95 = 244). Now EVERY player-facing HP figure is `HpDisplay.Scale(real, multiplier)` with the multiplier the fight uses: `PlayerCombatProfileCalculator.CombatPassives` is the ONE function that builds it (Resolve and `HpMultiplier` both call it, so what is shown and what fights cannot drift). Screens: the profile (`combat.CombatCurrentHp/MaxHp`), the solo victory (`AdventureModule.ShownHpLine(player, state.Passives.MaxHpMultiplier)`: the database HP after the level-up, scaled with the fight's own factor), the `/autohunt` victory and its "muy herido" message, `/heal` (also "Recuperaste N": the difference of the two shown figures), `/use` outside a fight, the tavern's "Curarme del todo" and the new-character card. Outside a fight the factor needs the player's blessings, so `ExecuteHealAsync/CoreAsync`, `ExecuteUseAsync`, `AutoHuntModule.ExecuteAsync` and `TabernaModule.ExecuteActionAsync` take a trailing optional `IPlayerBonusService? bonusService` (the modules inject it); without it (old harnesses) the factor comes from the player's row alone — class and Vida enchant, no blessing. **The database still stores REAL HP** (`ToDbHpDelta` and the combat math are untouched). **Healing and levelling use the SAME units (second half of v0.25.3, the owner: "que haga la cuenta antes si es esa clase… que quede todo óptimo")**: a food that says "cura 15 HP" heals 15 of the HP you SEE, as it always did inside a fight (where the HP is already in those units), so outside a fight `/heal` plans in shown units (`HealPlanner.Plan(shownMissing, …)`; "Curarme del todo" fills the bar completely so rounding never leaves one short) and passes the database the equivalent REAL HP (`HpDisplay.ToReal`: 15 shown = 16 real for a Ninja ×0.95, 13 for a Warrior ×1.2, away-from-zero rounding, never 0); `/use` outside a fight does the same; the level-up banner's "Vida máxima +N" is `HpDisplay.Scale(15 × levels, factor)` (`LevelUpCard.Compose(..., hpMultiplier)`, filled by `ProgressNotifier`, which now also takes optional `IUserRepository` and `IPlayerBonusService`: Ninja +14, Warrior +18). **Economy side effect, deliberate**: damage taken is in shown units for every class (monster hits are absolute), so healing in shown units makes refilling cost the same food for the same damage; before, out-of-fight food healed REAL HP, a hidden 20 % discount for the Warrior (×1.2) and 15 % for the Archer, and a 5 % surcharge for the Ninja. Warriors and Archers now eat a bit more to refill. To undo it, go back to real units in `realHeal` (`TavernModule.ExecuteHealCoreAsync`) and in the `RestoreHpAsync` call of `UseModule`. Harnesses that heal pin their user to a Hechicero (factor 1.0) so food heals what it says. **Measured when the owner asked if the Ninja with Esquive and the Mage were broken (`tools/balance2 -- L 5 <class>`)**: see `MEJORAS.md` — Esquive V on the Ninja equals Filo V (30 % defeat against the Zone 5 boss at min+3, unenchanted 42.5 %, Guarda V 13 %) and the Mage's edge is the LIFE its Sifón saves (61–73 % of the bar per boss against 85–96 % of the others), not its win rate.

**Fight XP ×0.5 (v0.18.1; `CombatRewardCalculator.FightXpScale`; NO migration, no data change)** — the owner: "el boss de la zona 3 para pasar a la 4 está muy fácil con el equipo de zona 2… capaz subirle la vida o el daño"; then, on seeing the numbers, "puede ser que se suba de nivel muy rápido" → "0,33 es una banda, dejémoslo en 0,5". Measured with the real resolver (scratchpad `balance2` part J, 4 classes, ability + one food): the Zone 3 boss with the Zone 2 class weapon + amulet is a wall at EQUAL level (defeat 99 % at level 10, 95 % at 13, 78 % at 16, 39 % at 20, 4 % at 25, 0 % from 30; at level 60 it costs 8 % of the life) and it is not softer than the other bosses, so it was never the boss: the player arrives with 15+ levels too many. With perfect play (hunt every minute, travel every 30 min ×30, boss every 5 h ×15, always in the highest zone the level allows; an optimistic bound, the same assumption as the 31 h of the five-zone class set) the level was **22 at 6 h, 36 at 12 h, 49 at 20 h and 60 at 31 h**, while the zone ladder asks for levels 1/5/10/15/20 (the Fogón 25): level stopped gating after ~7 h and any boss could be walked over with the previous gear. **A flat boss buff was rejected**: it does not bother an over-levelled player (HP ×1.25 + damage ×1.15 still loses 0 % from level 40) and it punishes who did it right (Zone 3 own gear at level 13: 23 % → 87 % defeat). The fix: the XP of EVERY monster fight (hunt, travel, boss, raid, `/autohunt`; they all go through `CombatRewardCalculator.Roll`) is multiplied by 0.5 BEFORE the player bonuses (pets, Fuego Nuevo, blessings multiply on top). Result with the same model: level **13 / 22 / 33 / 43 at 6 / 12 / 20 / 31 h** (0.33 would be 9 / 15 / 25 / 34). Gold, drops and the boss chest do not move; missions, achievements, `/daily` and the Arena prize pay their own XP unscaled (so they weigh relatively more now); nobody loses a level (the stored level and XP stay; only new XP is slower). Side effects: reaching level 25 for the Fogón goes from ~7 h to ~14 h of perfect play (the Fogón gear still takes longer), and the death penalty, which is the whole level's XP, is worth twice as many hunts. Not built (offered): boss stats that scale with the player's level above the zone's minimum (the real lever for a level-60 player, but it changes every boss and raid). If it is retuned, redo the level-per-hour table and part J.

**Zone difficulty is a ladder, and it is calibrated, not guessed.** All 5 zones are loaded. Each zone needs a *jump*
in gear, ×1.6 the previous one (class weapon ATQ per zone: Z1 +14, Z2 +30, Z3 +48, Z4 +75, Z5 +120 — the old base 9/20/32/50/80 × 1.5, baked into the number in v0.27.0 when the class-synergy multiplier was removed (`rebalance` details: `Database/remove_synergy_and_general_weapons.sql`); the single amulet's DEF is **10/35/60/85/120 since v0.15.1** (it was 10/18/30/46/75, the "low" amulets the ladder was calibrated with — see "Amulets and defense" below) — all in `rework_drops_and_recipes.sql`), and its monsters are
tuned so that **entering** with the previous zone's gear costs (~5 turns, ~47% of HP in a common fight), the zone's
own gear leaves commons at 13-18% HP (comfortable, never a walkover), and the **boss** is the exam (67-87% defeat
with the previous zone's gear, ~16% with its own). Numbers come from a simulation that drives the real
`CombatTurnResolver`: fix a nominal, strictly increasing gear ladder, then bisect the monsters' HP and damage factors
until the entry fight hits those targets (the simulator is `tools/balance2` since v0.18.3; the ladder-solving bisection was a one-off script that is NOT in the repo — rebuild it; the sensitivity to
remember is that the "exit" target controls how fast the ladder inflates, because each zone is entered with the
previous zone's exit gear). Never retune a zone's monsters without its gear (recipes) shipping with it, or it becomes
a wall, and re-measure that zone's raid when its boss changes. The whole ladder is the **run-1 baseline**: a future
post-Zone-5 reset will raise drop % and material quantities, so don't lower recipes for run 1 because they're slow.
Recipes (stats, gold, ingredients) are owned by `rework_drops_and_recipes.sql`, which deletes the old ingredients of the 30 recipes before loading, so a changed recipe never keeps stale rows.
