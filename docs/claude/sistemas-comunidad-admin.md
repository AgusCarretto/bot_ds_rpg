# Mini-evento, recordatorios, voto, top.gg, admin, códigos, invitaciones

> Texto movido literal desde el CLAUDE.md viejo el 2026-11-12 (en inglés, como estaba). El índice está en CLAUDE.md.

**Mini-event (`Services/MiniEventService.cs`, `GameData/MiniEvents.cs`, `Modules/MiniEventModule.cs`)** — after a slash or text command in a guild channel, with
a small chance (default 3%, at most one per channel every 30 min, one open at a time) the bot posts "a miner dropped a bag of stones / a woodcutter's bundle
came loose / a traveller's pocket tore" with a "¡Juntar!" button; whoever clicks within ~15 s joins once, then every participant gets a SMALL reward that
**grows with the number of participants** (stones/wood: 2 units + 2 per extra person, cap 10 people; silver: N "hunts of gold" of the player's own zone).
Same concurrency idea as the raid: state mutated under a lock with no `await`, the window closes inside the lock (no late joins, no double pay), payment
(`IMiniEventRepository.PayAsync`, one transaction per participant) happens outside it; in-memory, a restart just makes the button say "ya terminó".
Tunable by env vars (`MiniEvent__ChancePercent`, `MiniEvent__ChannelCooldownMinutes`, `MiniEvent__JoinSeconds`); to test it set chance 100 and cooldown 0.

**Cooldown reminders (v0.16.0; `/reminders`, `aa recordatorios|reminders|avisos`; `GameData/Reminders.cs`, `Services/ReminderService.cs` + `ReminderScheduler.cs`, `Repositories/ReminderRepository.cs`, `Modules/ReminderModule.cs`, `Database/add_reminders.sql` — run it on a live database BEFORE the v0.16.0 binary: every command now reads and writes `reminders`)** — the owner: "armar un reminder dentro del mismo bot" ("hunt listo", "ya puedes ir de caseria nuevamente"; the same for travel, chop, mine, boss/raid, box, pet feed). When a wait ends the bot posts, in THE CHANNEL where the command was used, one line that pings the player. Twelve kinds in `ReminderCatalog.All` (the twelfth, `dragon`, came with v0.33.0, see "the dragon"; the eleventh, `vote`, came with v0.18.0 and is also a `cooldowns` key — see "Voting"; the original ten: `hunt`, `travel`, `chop`, `mine`, `boss`, `buybox`, `chop_adv`, `mine_adv`, `pet`, `daily`: the first eight are the keys of the `cooldowns` table, the last two live elsewhere); a new one is a row there plus its `WaitOf`.
**Nothing about "when" is stored twice.** After every slash command, button and `aa` command (`NoticeDelivery.Attach` — the same `InteractionExecuted` handler that delivers notices, after the delivery —, and `Program.HandleTextMessageAsync`) `ReminderService.SyncAsync(user, channel, hint)` re-reads the REAL state: ALL the player's `cooldowns` rows (one query, `ICooldownRepository.GetAllAsync`; `ReminderCatalog.FromCooldowns` keeps those still running, due = last execution + the game's own duration from `CooldownCatalog`, so a boss lost with its 30-minute retry already comes shifted in `last_executed_at`), the pets only when the command hint mentions `pet`/`mascota` (`PetDue`: when the FIRST pet that can still level up can eat again, with the player's own Buen Pienso cooldown) and the daily only when it mentions `daily`/`diario` (24 h after the last claim). The queue is the `reminders` table, ONE row per (player, kind): a new command overwrites it. The upsert only touches a row if its `due_at` CHANGED (`ON CONFLICT ... WHERE due_at <> EXCLUDED.due_at`), so the dozens of button clicks of one fight write nothing and a command from another channel cannot steal the notice of a cooldown already running. `reminder_settings.off_kinds` holds what the player turned OFF (an empty/missing row = everything on; a future kind starts on); turning one off also deletes its pending row.
**The clock** (`ReminderScheduler`, a 5-second `PeriodicTimer` started in `ReadyAsync` like `ArenaScheduler`): `IReminderRepository.ClaimDueAsync` is a single `DELETE ... WHERE due_at <= now RETURNING` (a reminder leaves the queue exactly once even with two clocks — tested with 8 in parallel), groups by (player, channel) and sends ONE message per group: a single reminder is one line (`<@id> 🏹 ¡Cacería lista!...`), several are a header plus a line each. All the "do not notify" rules live in `ReminderScheduler.DeliverAsync`: `ReminderPolicy.IsEligible` (today everybody — **the owner said that a membership will exist later, and THIS is the one place to decide who gets reminders**), stale ones (`ReminderCatalog.IsStale`: a short wait overdue by 5 minutes or a long one by 6 hours — the bot was off — is dropped, never a burst on restart) and the SHORT kinds (hunt, chop, mine: `ReminderKind.AutoDelete` = 2 minutes, the message deletes itself) are skipped if the player is in a fight/raid or used a command in the last 10 s (`ActiveWindow`: no point reminding someone who is already hitting the button). A send that fails (deleted channel, lost permission) logs a warning and is dropped. `Sender` is a test seam. **Raids**: only the player who lands the last blow has an interaction to sync from, so `RaidSession.ChannelId` (set where the lobby is created) and the static `RaidModule.Reminders` (assigned in `ReadyAsync`; null in the harnesses) re-sync EVERY participant at activation and at victory/wipe, because that is when their `boss` cooldown changes.
**The screen** (`/reminders`, ephemeral; `ReminderModule.Compose` is pure): one line per kind (✅ on / 🔕 struck through, with "cada 1 min" read from the game's constants), a multi-select `reminders_pick:{owner}` where the CHECKED options are the ones you receive (min 0 — checking nothing silences everything), and two buttons `reminders_all:{owner}:on|off`; no emoji inside the options (the usual rule). Not built on purpose: a DM fallback when the channel is gone, per-kind channels, and a quiet-hours window. Harness: `remindertest` (≈90 checks: the pure rules, the screen, the repository and service against the real database with disposable users, the clock with a fake sender, `NoticeDelivery.Attach` driven through the real `InteractionService`, the raid hook, routing, DI and help).

**Voting (v0.18.0; `/vote`, `aa vote|votar|voto`; `GameData/Vote.cs`, `Services/TopGgVoteClient.cs`, `Repositories/VoteRepository.cs`, `Modules/VoteModule.cs`; NO migration)** — the owner: "que poniendo `aa vote` te dé un mensajito de lo que te da el vote, que es una caja (Baúl de Roble), y te resetea el boss". One command for everything: with no vote to collect it says what voting gives (1 Baúl de Roble + the boss/raid cooldown wiped) and where to vote; with an active, uncollected top.gg vote it pays it. **The bot never receives a webhook and opens no port**: when someone types `/vote` it asks top.gg `GET https://top.gg/api/v1/projects/@me/votes/{discordId}?source=discord` with `Authorization: Bearer <project token>` (verified against docs.top.gg on 2026-10-16: 200 `{created_at, expires_at, weight}`, 404 = never voted or expired). `TopGgVoteClient` never throws (a failure is `Unavailable` and `/vote` says "no pude confirmar tu voto… no se perdió nada"), caches a person's answer 5 s, times out at 6 s and **never prints the token** (the harness checks the log). **A vote is collected exactly once**: the `cooldowns` row `vote` stores the `created_at` of the last COLLECTED vote and `VoteRepository.ClaimAsync` is ONE transaction: `INSERT … ON CONFLICT DO UPDATE … WHERE cooldowns.last_executed_at < EXCLUDED` (the guard: the same or an older vote returns no row = `AlreadyClaimed`), the box into the inventory (a missing `Baúl de Roble` rolls EVERYTHING back = `BoxMissing`, the vote is not marked) and a `DELETE` of the `boss` cooldown row (Boss and Raid are one cooldown; it reports `BossReset` only if it was still running) — 20 simultaneous claims of the same vote pay once. So `last + 12 h` (`VoteRules.Interval`) is when top.gg lets you vote again, which is what `/cd` and the `vote` reminder show; collection itself does not depend on that duration. **Since 2026-10-31 players vote for the BOT** (top.gg approved it; vote link `https://top.gg/bot/1544372330157834272/vote`; the token is the bot project's v1 token with **Read** permission only, the bot never writes to top.gg and needs no webhook; verified with `GET /projects/@me` → `"type": "bot"` and a real vote collected with `aa vote`). From 2026-10-16 to then it was the SERVER project: top.gg's v1 API calls both a "project" (`type` = `bot`/`server`/`game`, same `/projects/@me/votes/{id}` route), so the switch was only the two variables `TopGg__Token` and `TopGg__VoteUrl` in Railway, no code; each project keeps its own votes (votes for the server no longer pay). The texts still say "top.gg", not "el bot", and can stay that way. **Dormant by design**: no `TopGg__Token` means `/vote` only describes the reward, `/cd` hides the line (`CooldownCatalog.Vote` is deliberately NOT in `All`; `BuildStatusEmbedAsync(..., voteEnabled)`), and nothing is paid; setting `TopGg__Token` (and `TopGg__VoteUrl` for the link) in Railway and restarting turns it on, no code. **Economy** (`report_economy.sql`): a Roble is ~8.2k of gold-equivalent and the boss reset is one extra repeat-box win (a Zone 5 Arcón ~43k), so a vote every 12 h about doubles the free box supply of every zone (the free week today is 13k / 100k / 100k / 512k / 512k) and lowers the ≥ 20.8 h floor to the Zone 5 boss to ~11 h (gear still limits a lap at ~31 h): if it turns out too strong, the levers are collecting the reset once a day or changing `VoteRules.RewardBoxName`. A vote that is not collected before it expires (~12 h) is lost. Event `vote` (`amount` = boxes, `detail` = `boss_reset`/`boss_ready`); no achievement or mission uses it. Harness: `votetest` (69 checks: the parser, the client against a fake HTTP server — route, token header, cache, 401/500/garbage/timeout/closed port, no token in the log —, the repository with the real database and the 20-way race, the screens, `/cd`, reminders, routing, help and DI).

**The top.gg page is kept in the repo (since 2026-10-31; `docs/topgg/descripcion.md`, `docs/topgg/anuncios.md`, `docs/topgg/comandos.md`)** — the owner: "quiero tener todo bastante actualizado y ser constante con eso". The headline and the long description live in `descripcion.md`, the player command list in `comandos.md` (to read) and `comandos.json` (what top.gg's *Commands → Import from Discord* accepts: Discord's command JSON, regenerated from the code with `dotnet run --project tools/slashjson -- docs/topgg/comandos.json`, no token needed; a new or renamed slash command means regenerating it and updating the table), and one announcement per DAY at most in `anuncios.md` (the owner, 2026-11-05: «si los anuncios son 1 por día entonces complica»: when several versions go out the same day they are MERGED into ONE entry with everything the player notices, most important first, dated that day; newest first, title + text, written for players in Spanish; only the last one is pinned on the page, so each must stand alone). **When a release changes something players notice, add it to that day's announcement in the same session (merging, never a second entry for the same date) and update the description if a feature list in it changed**; the owner copies them to top.gg (panel → *Announcements* / *Information*). Do not announce internal fixes, and keep pet-expedition prizes secret there too (see v0.39.1).

**The admin command: `aa admingive` (v0.34.0; `Modules/AdminGiveHandler.cs`, `Modules/TextCommandModule.Admin.cs`, `GameData/AdminGive.cs`, `Services/AdminAccess.cs`, `Repositories/AdminRepository.cs`; NO migration; harness `admintest`)** — the owner (2026-10-28): "necesito poder dar cosas para probar, plata sin tenerla, cajas, items… y para sorteos, giveaways de cajas u oro… un comando solo para mí que es el admin, distinto a give, así no se pierde nada del juego". It CREATES things (it does not move them like `/give`), so it is built defensively: **(1) who**: ONLY the Discord ids in the environment variable `Admin__UserIds` (comma separated; `AdminAccess`, read from `IConfiguration["Admin:UserIds"]`); no id is written in the code or the repo, and without the variable there are no admins and the command is off. Anyone else gets NO answer at all (it does not even confirm the command exists; the attempt is logged as `[ADMIN] rechazado`); the only exception is "there is no admin configured at all", where it says which variable is missing. The id comes from the gateway message, not from anything the user types. **(2) text only, on purpose**: no slash version and no `[Summary]`, so it appears in no command list and nobody else can see it; blocked mid-fight like every command that is not in the allow-list. **(3) what it gives** (`aa admingive <what> <amount> [@someone]`, no mention = to yourself; amounts accept `1.500`, `10k`, `1m` through `AmountParser`, "all" is refused): `gold` (`oro`), `dust` (`polvo`), `xp` (`exp`, through `IProgressionRepository.AddXpAsync`, so it can level up) and `item <name…> [quantity]` (any catalog item by name, accent- and case-insensitive; an unknown name answers "¿quisiste decir…?" with up to 8 catalog names that contain what was typed). Boxes, materials, food, eggs, pet food and dragon food go to the BAG; a **weapon or amulet is PUT ON** (replaces the worn piece and starts without enchant, like forging it) and a **tool is PUT ON** in its slot (axe/pickaxe, replacing the old one even if it is worse: it is for testing); for those the quantity does not count. Limits per command: gold and Polvo up to `int.MaxValue`, XP 100 M, 100,000 units of an item; the repository CLAMPS the total at `int.MaxValue` in SQL (`LEAST(old::bigint + n, 2147483647)`) instead of failing with "integer out of range". **(4) it never creates accounts** (a target without `/start` gets a message and nothing) and each give is ONE statement or ONE transaction that locks the user row (10 simultaneous gives sum exactly). **(5) audit trail**: a `[ADMIN] actor -> target: what` line in the server log and an `admin_give` event in `game_events` (`amount` = quantity, `detail` = what and who gave it), which is what to read to check a giveaway; the reply mentions only users (`AllowedMentions(Users)`, never `@everyone` or roles) so the winner of a giveaway gets pinged. To enable it locally put `Admin__UserIds=<id>` in `.env`; in Railway add the same variable (Raw Editor) and restart. **Not built**: several winners in one command, a slash version, giving a dragon or setting the PLAYER's level (the owner can buy a dragon with the gold and level with the XP). **`aa adminset` (v0.48.1; `Modules/AdminSetHandler.cs`, `GameData/AdminSet.cs`, `IAdminRepository.SetDragonTierAsync/SetDragonLevelAsync`, event `admin_set`; harness `admintest` section 5)** — the owner (2026-11-04): a friend's dragon got stuck after its tier was changed by hand at the moment it met the minimum attempts, and he asked for «un `aa adminset` con `settier`/`setlevel` del dragón, de 1 a 10». Same gate, same shape and same audit trail as `aa admingive` (text only, no `[Summary]`, `Admin__UserIds`, silent for anyone else, never creates an account or a dragon). `aa adminset dragon tier 5 [@someone]` sets the TIER (1-10, no breeding roll); because the training level is capped at tier × 10 and the pity counters belong to the old tier's odds, it **clips the level to the new tier's cap and zeroes BOTH fail streaks** (`fail_streak` and `solo_fail_streak`), and leaves the type, the name and the 12-hour breeding rest alone. `aa adminset dragon level 40 [@someone]` sets the TRAINING level (0 up to the current tier's cap); above the cap it refuses and says the cap instead of clipping silently. The tier and the level are written under the dragon row's `FOR UPDATE`, so a burst of both keeps `level <= tier × 10` (tested with 24 at once). `AdminGive.SplitTarget` (the mention reader) is shared by both commands. To fix a dragon on a live database without a deploy, the same effect is `UPDATE player_dragons SET tier = N, level = LEAST(level, N * 10), fail_streak = 0, solo_fail_streak = 0 WHERE discord_id = <id>;`.

**Redeemable codes (v0.37.0; `aa code <CÓDIGO>` and `/code`, the admin panel `aa admincode`; `GameData/RedeemCodes.cs`, `Repositories/RedeemCodeRepository.cs`, `Modules/CodeHandler.cs` + `CodeModule.cs` + `TextCommandModule.Code.cs`, `Database/add_redeem_codes.sql` — OPTIONAL before the binary: without the three tables only `aa code` and `aa admincode` fail; harness `codetest`)** — the owner (2026-10-28): "vamos a ir trabajando en code, `aa code`: un comando nuevo que tenga códigos que se puedan canjear por cosas" (giveaways, launch gifts, events). **What it is**: a code (3-32 chars of A-Z 0-9 `_` `-`, typed in any case and with accents: `RedeemCodeRules.Normalize`) pays a LIST of rewards when a player redeems it — gold, Polvo, EXP (it can level up and rings the usual banner through the `level_up` event) and any BAG item (boxes, food, materials, eggs, pet food, dragon food…; NOT weapons, amulets or tools: `CodeHandler` refuses them at creation) — with an optional cap of how many people can redeem it (`max_uses`) and an optional expiry. **One redemption per person** (primary key `(code_id, discord_id)` in `redeem_code_claims`). Tables: `redeem_codes` (the code and its rules, `uses` counter, `active`), `redeem_code_rewards` (kind gold|dust|xp|item, amount, item), `redeem_code_claims`. **Redeeming is ONE transaction** (`RedeemCodeRepository.RedeemAsync`): lock the player row, then the code row (`FOR UPDATE`, in that order everywhere), check active / expired / cap, insert the claim (`ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING`: two simultaneous requests of the same person pay once), `uses + 1`, pay every reward (gold and Polvo clamped at `int.MaxValue` with `LEAST(x::bigint + n, 2147483647)`, EXP through `LevelingApplier`); any failure rolls back everything, and a code with no rewards left (their items were deleted from the catalog, `ON DELETE CASCADE`) answers `NoRewards` WITHOUT burning anyone's claim. 12 people redeeming a cap-5 code at once pay exactly 5 (tested). Never creates accounts. **Players**: `/code codigo:` answers PRIVATELY (a typed code in a channel is readable by everybody; the typed `aa code|codigo|código|redeem|cupon CÓDIGO` works too and the bot tries to delete the player's message if it has permission — `canjear` belongs to the drop exchange, do not reuse it). **Since 2026-11-04 the typed `aa code` answers by DIRECT MESSAGE** (the owner, seeing a redemption embed with the code and the prizes in the channel while he was giving the codes away as a discovery game: «el mensaje de canje debería ser para vos solo»; text commands cannot be ephemeral): `CodeHandler.DeliverTypedAsync` sends the answer, success or error alike, to the player's DMs and leaves in the channel only «🎟️ <@id> Te respondí por mensaje privado.», which says neither the code nor the prizes nor whether it worked; if the DM fails (closed DMs) the redemption is already paid, so the channel gets the prizes WITHOUT the code's name plus a line pointing at `/code`; typed inside a DM with the bot it answers there. `CodeReply.EmbedWithoutCode` carries that variant. `/code` was always private. Harness `codetest` section 8c. **No brake on wrong guesses since v0.43.1** (the owner, 2026-10-31: «que la gente pruebe el code que quiera», for a Halloween code given as a riddle): until then `RedeemAttemptLimiter` blocked 10 minutes after 5 non-existent codes in 10 minutes; it was deleted. A code is still paid once per person, and guessing an unannounced one at random is hopeless. Messages and the redeemed embed are translated (`Locales/en.json`); `/code` is NOT in the fight-lock allow-list (blocked mid-fight). **Admin panel** (`aa admincode`, text only, no `[Summary]`, same `Admin__UserIds` gate as `aa admingive`: anyone else gets NO answer, the attempt is logged as `[ADMIN] rechazado`): `create CODIGO [100] [7d] | gold 5k | item Cofre de Oro 1 | dust 200 | xp 500` (people and duration optional and in any order, up to 6 rewards after `|`, same amount syntax as `aa admingive`: 1.500, 10k, 1m), `list` (the 15 newest), `info CODIGO`, `off CODIGO` / `on CODIGO` (switch a code off without deleting it), and since v0.43.2 `edit CODIGO [people] [duration] | rewards…` (same syntax as create; it REPLACES the cap, the duration counted from now and the whole reward list, keeps who already redeemed it and whether it is on) and `delete CODIGO` (only a code nobody redeemed, guarded in the `DELETE … WHERE uses = 0`, so its name is free again; a redeemed one must be switched off, or deleting it would forget who redeemed it). The owner asked for them after creating HALLOWEEN and CALABAZA by mistake on 2026-10-31. Events: `code_redeem` (detail = the code) and `admin_code` (what the admin did: the audit trail for giveaways). **Not built, on purpose or for later**: codes per class/level/zone, a code that gives equipment or a pet, per-server codes, a "my redeemed codes" list, deleting codes (turn them off).

**Inviting friends (v0.43.0; `/invitedby amigo:`, `aa invitedby|invited|invitado|meinvito|referido [@amigo]`; `GameData/Referrals.cs`, `Repositories/ReferralRepository.cs`, `Modules/ReferralModule.cs` + `TextCommandModule.Referral.cs`, the level-5 hook in `Services/GameEventService.cs`, `Database/add_referrals.sql` — OPTIONAL before the binary: without the table only `aa invitedby` fails and the level-5 hook logs a warning; harness `referraltest`)** — the owner (2026-10-31), after announcing by hand "20k when your invitee does /start, 80k more at level 5" (paid with `aa admingive`): "después del tutorial: si te invitó un amigo, escribí aa invitedby y el nombre de tu amigo así le damos un premio… solo 1 validez por cuenta", plus 10k for the new player; then "que cualquiera lo pueda hacer pero 1 SOLA vez… si ya pasó el nivel 5 o de FN, que le dé los 80k también instantáneo" (the old players are few and he does not mind them gifting each other some gold, so there is NO "new account only" rule and NO block on two players naming each other). **Rules** (`ReferralRules`): any account with a character names who invited it ONCE (`referrals.invitee_id` is the primary key: 10 simultaneous requests of the same invitee register once, tested); the invitee gets **10,000** gold and the inviter **20,000** in the same transaction (both user rows locked `FOR UPDATE` ordered by `discord_id`, gold clamped at `int.MaxValue`); the inviter gets **80,000 more** when the invitee "reaches the goal" = level ≥ 5 OR at least one Fuego Nuevo (`ReachedGoal`) — **right away if the invitee already had** (so 100,000 at once; `level_paid_at` is written in the insert), otherwise when `GameEventService.RecordAsync` sees a `level_up` of that player: `IReferralRepository.PayLevelBonusAsync` is ONE statement (`UPDATE referrals … WHERE level_paid_at IS NULL AND (level >= 5 OR fuego_nuevo > 0)` in a CTE that then adds the gold), so it pays once (8 simultaneous level-ups → one payment, tested). Every XP source already records `level_up`, so nothing else needs a hook; `GameEventService` takes the repository as an OPTIONAL third constructor argument (old harnesses build it without one and nothing is paid). Refused before touching anything: a bot, yourself, an inviter without a character (it never creates accounts). **Screens**: the reply is public and pings the inviter (the mention goes in the message content, `AllowedMentions(Users)`); `aa invitedby` with nobody shows who invited you, how many you invited and how many of them reached level 5; the inviter and the invitee get a private notice when the 80k are paid (the inviter's arrives with their next command, in memory like every notice). The hint "¿Te invitó un amigo?" is the last field of `/tutorial` and of the new-character card of `/start` (`ReferralModule.Hint`), and `/invitedby` is in the "Con otros jugadores" line of `/info`. Events (on the INVITEE): `referral` (`amount` = gold paid to the inviter, `detail` = the inviter's id) and `referral_level`. All texts are translated (`Locales/en.json`). **Not built**: invite links or codes, any limit on how many people one player invites, a leaderboard of inviters, achievements or titles for inviting. If alts become a problem, the levers are the amounts and the goal level in `ReferralRules`; the `referral` events say who invited whom.

**The random-events channel (v0.56.2; `MiniEventSettings.For`, `Services/MiniEventService.cs`; NO migration; harness `stage8test`)** — the owner's idea of 2026-11-06 («un canal para alguien que quiera quedarse ahí farmeando»), built on 2026-11-13. Mini-events still spawn where people play; if the environment variable `MiniEvent__EventChannelId` holds a channel id, in THAT channel they spawn more often: `MiniEvent__EventChannelChancePercent` (default **15 %** per command, against 3 % elsewhere) and at most one every `MiniEvent__EventChannelCooldownMinutes` (default **5**, against 30). Everything else is unchanged (the same small, capped rewards, 15 s to join, one open event per channel, no pings: the channel itself is the notice). Without the variable nothing changes. To turn it on: create the channel, copy its id (Developer Mode → right click → Copy ID), add `MiniEvent__EventChannelId=<id>` in Railway (Raw Editor) and restart. A mini-event only spawns after a COMMAND in that channel, so people have to play there.
